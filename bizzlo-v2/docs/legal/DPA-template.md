# Bizzlo Data Processing Addendum Template

This draft is a launch placeholder and must be reviewed by counsel before signing.

## Parties

Controller: Partner consultancy or Videshway, depending on the student relationship.

Processor/Subprocessor: Bizzlo service stack operated by Videshway with Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, Resend, Sentry, and PostHog as configured subprocessors.

## Processing

Purpose: admissions workflow management, document handling, partner account management, audit logging, and support.

Categories: student identity/contact data, academic records, financial documents, partner user records, application workflow records, and audit metadata.

## Security Measures

Role-based access, RLS, private storage, OTP-protected public upload links, upload caps, audit trails, backups, and incident-response procedures.

## Data Rights

Exports and erasure are handled through `docs/DATA_EXPORT.md` and `docs/DATA_DELETION.md`.
